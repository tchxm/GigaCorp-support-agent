## SHIPPING POLICY
GigaCorp offers three shipping tiers for all domestic orders:
- Standard Shipping (5-7 business days): Free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99.
- Express Shipping (2-3 business days): $12.99 flat rate.
- Overnight Shipping (next business day): $24.99 flat rate.
International shipping is available to 40+ countries, including India, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, Germany, France, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates. GigaCorp ships to India for a $19.99 flat international shipping rate, with typical delivery in 10-15 business days after shipment.
International orders receive tracking within 24 hours of shipment. Customs duties, import taxes, and local clearance fees are the customer's responsibility. We do NOT ship to sanctioned countries including North Korea, Iran, Syria, Cuba, and the Crimea region.
All orders receive a tracking number via email within 24 hours of shipment. Oversized items (over 70 lbs or 108 inches combined length+girth) incur a $35 surcharge. Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST on business days ship the same day; orders placed after 2:00 PM ship the next business day.
Shipping delays caused by carrier issues, weather, or customs are outside GigaCorp's control and are not eligible for refunds of shipping fees.

## RETURN AND REFUND PROCESS
GigaCorp accepts returns within 30 days of delivery for most products. Items must be unused, in original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase (order confirmation email or packing slip).
To initiate a return, contact our support team or use the self-service portal at returns.gigacorp.com. You will receive a pre-paid return label via email. Drop the package at any carrier location.
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a GigaCorp error (wrong item, defective product).
Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged. This includes clearance items, personalized products, and gift cards. Opened software and digital licenses are also non-returnable.
For defective products, contact support within 14 days of delivery. We will either replace the item at no cost or issue a full refund including original shipping charges.

## BUSINESS HOURS
GigaCorp customer support is available during the following hours (all times Eastern):
- Monday through Friday: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM EST
- Saturday: 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM EST
- Sunday: Closed
Live chat and phone support follow these hours. Email support is monitored during business hours with a target response time of 4 hours.
During major US holidays (New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day), support is closed. Emails received on holidays are answered the next business day.
Self-service options (order tracking, return initiation, FAQ) are available 24/7 on our website.

## SERVICE TIERS
GigaCorp offers three customer service tiers based on annual spending:
- Bronze (default): Standard support via email and chat. Response time target: 4 hours during business hours. No dedicated representative.
- Silver ($1,000+ annual spend): Priority queue for chat and phone. Response time target: 1 hour during business hours. Access to extended return windows (45 days instead of 30). Free standard shipping on all orders.
- Gold ($5,000+ annual spend): Dedicated account representative available via direct phone line and email. Response time target: 15 minutes during business hours. Extended return window of 60 days. Free express shipping on all orders. Early access to new product launches and exclusive Gold-member promotions.
Tier status is evaluated quarterly based on rolling 12-month spend. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next quarter. Upgrades are applied immediately upon qualifying.
To check your current tier, log in to your account at account.gigacorp.com or ask any support representative.
